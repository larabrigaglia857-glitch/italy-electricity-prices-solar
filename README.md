# Who Pays for Italy's Energy Transition?
Electricity prices, solar power and the cost for businesses
Italian electiricy prices, solar power and the impact on businesses.



## Business questions

1. How has the Italian electricity price changed from 2019 to today?
2. How many hours per year does solar push prices down, and by how much?
3. How much does the gas price drive the electricity price?
4. How much would a company save by shifting consumption to solar hours?



## Data sources 

* GME (Italian power Exchange): day-ahead prices
* Terna: generation by source
* TTF: natural gas price
